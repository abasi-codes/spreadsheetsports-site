What do these stats mean?
GGames
Games played in the regular season. A full season is 82. Players need 65 to qualify for MVP and All-NBA.
GSGames started
Games he was in the starting lineup. Close to G means a full-time starter.
MINMinutes per game
Average minutes on the court per game. 30+ is a heavy starter's load. Under 20 is a bench role.
PTSPoints per game
Points scored per game. 20+ is a top scorer: 47 of 379 rotation players got there in 2025-26.
REBRebounds per game
Offensive plus defensive rebounds per game. 10+ is elite: only 13 rotation players averaged it in 2025-26.
ASTAssists per game
Passes that lead directly to a made basket, per game. 7+ is lead-playmaker territory: 14 players in 2025-26.
STLSteals per game
Times per game he takes the ball from the other team. 1.5+ is among the league leaders (20 players in 2025-26).
BLKBlocks per game
Opponent shots he blocks per game. 1.5+ is a real rim protector (17 players in 2025-26).
TOVTurnovers per game
Times per game he loses the ball to the other team. Lower is better, but stars who run the offense often average 3+.
FG%Field-goal percentage
Share of his shots that go in (2s and 3s). Depends on shot type: bigs near the rim often shoot 55%+, guards around 45%.
3P%Three-point percentage
Share of his three-point attempts that go in. League average was 36.0% in 2025-26. 38%+ on real volume is very good.
FT%Free-throw percentage
Share of his free throws that go in. 80% is solid, 90% is elite.
MPMinutes played
Total minutes on the court that season. 2,000+ is a full-time starter. Percentiles use players with 500+.
BPMBox plus/minus
Points per 100 possessions he adds compared with an average player, estimated from the box score. 0 = average, −2 ≈ replacement level, +4.3 = the typical 2025-26 All-Star, +8 = MVP level.
VORPValue over replacement player
BPM turned into a season total, so it rewards playing a lot of minutes. 1 ≈ solid rotation player, 2+ ≈ good starter, 3.5 = the typical 2025-26 All-Star.
WSWin shares
An estimate of how many team wins he produced, offense plus defense. 8.0 = the typical 2025-26 All-Star. 12+ is MVP level.
WS/48Win shares per 48 minutes
Win shares per full game of playing time. League average is about .100. .200+ is elite.
TS%True shooting percentage
Scoring efficiency that counts 3s as worth more and includes free throws. League average was 58.2% in 2025-26. 62%+ is excellent, under 54% is inefficient.
USG%Usage rate
Share of his team's plays he finishes with a shot, free throws or a turnover while he's on the floor. The median rotation player is about 19%. Go-to stars are 28%+.
On/offOn/off net rating
How many points per 100 possessions better his team was with him on the court than off it. Noisy: it also depends on teammates and backups. ±5 or more is a big swing.
