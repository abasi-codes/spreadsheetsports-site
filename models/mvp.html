Method

What's inside

Project the season

Last three seasons weighted 3-2-1 with an age curve: impact, points, rebounds + assists, games.

Score it like a voter

Voters reward all-around stars, the best player on a top seed and win shares. They also remember last year.

Voter fatigue

Nobody wins three in a row anymore, and nobody 31+ has won since Nash. Both are in the model.

Last year's Finals

Stars from last season's Finals teams get a ×1.3 bump. It was the one late idea that held up in the backtest.

Simulate 10,000 seasons

Team records and stat lines vary in every run, and the model counts how often each player wins.

Tested & rejected

Ideas that failed

  • Extra credit for the scoring champ, triple-doubles, advanced-stat leaders or the best record
  • Punishing injury history, boosting young "breakout" candidates
  • Vegas win totals, Vegas MVP odds, one giant model that learns everything: all worse
  • Other voter fatigue: back-to-back, chasing a 4th, two of the last three
  • How well a player played last postseason (scoring, plus-minus): sharper odds, fewer winners
  • Blocks, steals and DPOY votes (defensive win shares did help)

The model's blind spot: leaps. Rose in 2011, Curry in 2015, Giannis in 2019 and Jokić in 2021 all came out of nowhere.
