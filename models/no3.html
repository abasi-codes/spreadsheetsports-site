How it works

Four universes

Universe 1

Threes worth two

Every made three just counts as two. Nothing else changes.

Universe 2

One step inside

Every three becomes a long two one step inside the line, at the team's long-two percentage.

Universe 3

Shots move inside

Those shots spread into the team's normal two-point mix and go in at its two-point percentage.

Universe 4Most realistic

Defenses pack the paint

Universe 3, plus no spacing: with no three to guard, every two gets harder.
