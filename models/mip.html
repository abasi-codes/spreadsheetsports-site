Why the model starts in 2008

The voters changed

There's no definition of "improved" anywhere, so the voters decide what it means. Since 2008 they've rewarded a player who was already pretty good and then became a star, so the model only learns from those 19 seasons.

How it was built

Five versions

Each version is backtested: it goes back to each of the 19 seasons from 2008 to 2026, sees only what was known on opening night, and I check how often its favourite actually won. It's like giving the model old exams with the answers covered up.

The cheat check

7 became 4

I picked version five's ideas while looking at all 19 seasons, so I don't fully trust the 7. I ran the whole lab again 19 times, each time hiding the season it was about to predict. Done that way, it picks 4 winners. That's the honest number, and it's the one the report card and the Vegas comparison below use.

An idea that died

Teammates don't split the vote

Two players on one team taking votes from each other matters for Sixth Man. Here it does nothing: 16 of the last 41 winners had a teammate who also got votes. A winning team didn't matter either.
