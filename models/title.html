Method

How a season is simulated

Project every team

Last season's net rating, adjusted for roster changes by each player's minutes and impact, plus playoff experience.

Play the real schedule

All 1,230 games. Each game's win chance comes from the rating gap plus about 2 points of home court.

Add October uncertainty

Every simulated season gives each team a random shake, because nobody knows exactly how good a team is before tip-off.

Play-in & playoffs

Real seeding rules, best-of-seven series with 2-2-1-1-1 home court. Then repeat 10,000 times.

Tested & rejected

Ideas that failed

  • Aging and health discounts
  • Rings already won
  • Roster continuity
  • Two-year net rating
  • Coaching experience
  • Defense-specific ratings

Each made the backtest worse or no better, so none of them made the final model.
