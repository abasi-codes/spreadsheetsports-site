How it was built

Five versions

Each version is backtested: it goes back to every season from 1985 to 2026, sees only what was known on opening night, and we check how often its favourite actually won. Like old exams with the answers covered up.

The rule

More games off the bench than started

That's the only eligibility rule. There's no 65-game rule for this award, so the model's job is to guess the role as much as the stats.

The cheat test

11 / 42 even with the answers

Given every team's real final record, something nobody knows in October, a preseason model still only picked 11 winners. The award is decided during the season.

Model vs Vegas

Did the favourite win?

Preseason odds only go back to 2017-18, so this is 9 seasons. The model's favourite won 3, the Vegas favourite 2, and the model gave the real winner 8.3% on average vs Vegas's 5.5%. To be fair: Vegas had the winner in its top three 5 times, the model only 3. Neither favourite has won since 2020-21, and the last three winners were 50-to-1 or longer.
