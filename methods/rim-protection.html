Rim protection

Blocks are the highlight, but they're not the whole job. The best rim protectors also stop shots from happening and make the ones that do happen miss. This number counts all of it: rim points saved per 100 opponent possessions.

In plain English

What it measures

Two things happen at the rim when a great big is on the floor. Opponents take fewer shots there (deterrence), and they make fewer of the ones they take (FG% saved). We measure both from every possession of the last two regular seasons, then turn them into points:

rim points saved per 100 = shots deterred × (1.31 − 1.00) + FG% saved × 25.6 rim attempts per 100

A rim shot is worth 1.31 points on average (65.5% in the restricted area); every other shot is worth about 1.00. So a deterred rim attempt saves about 0.31 points. 0 is league average, +2 is elite, +4 is historic.

Lineup-adjusted means we estimate every player at the same time from every 10-man combination on the floor (a ridge regression, the same idea as RAPM), so nobody gets credit just for sharing the floor with a great rim protector. Each defender's estimate is gently pulled toward what his block rate suggests, because blocks turn out to carry real signal about FG% at the rim.

Backtest

Graded the honest way

We graded the model the honest way. We built it on 2024-25 only, then asked it to predict 2025-26 lineups it had never seen. If it can't beat simple stats on games it hasn't seen, there's no reason to trust it.

Caveats

What it can't see

  • Team scheme. There's no team term, so scheme (drop vs switch, zone) is partly credited to players, and teammates who almost always share the floor split credit uncertainly.
  • Uncertainty is model-based. The ± band is a 90% interval conditional on the model's shrinkage; it understates true uncertainty for low-minute players, whose estimates lean on the block prior. Treat percentile differences under about 10 points as ties.
  • Deterrence is the noisiest part (year-to-year r 0.59), and it assumes a deterred shot becomes an average non-rim shot. Turnovers and kick-outs to corner threes aren't modeled separately.
  • Rim = restricted-area field-goal attempts. Rim fouls without a recorded shot are left out, which slightly undervalues bigs who foul.
  • Two seasons pooled (2024-25 + 2025-26, equal weight), so it doesn't capture aging or injury inside that window. The same number shows in both season views on player pages.
  • Not yet cross-checked against the NBA's tracking-based opponent FG% at the rim, by design (licensing).
  • Shown for players with 1,000+ minutes over the two seasons (405 players).
