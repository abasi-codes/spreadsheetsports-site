How to read a line
  • The line is a milestone, like "25+ points". For each player we show the milestone closest to a coin flip over his last 10 games, the way a fair market line sits near 50%. Turn on alt lines to also see the next milestone down.
  • Hit in 7 of last 10 games: his last 10 games, across seasons. The window is always 10. We never pick the window that makes the number look best.
  • vs opponent: every game against tonight's opponent in the last two seasons, up to 10.
  • Home / away: his last 10 games at home (or on the road), matching tonight's venue.
  • 🔥 in a row: how many straight games he's cleared it. Consistency (the default sort) pools the three rows, and pulls small samples toward 50%.
  • Once a game is final, each card shows what actually happened (✓ or ✗).

Players come from each team's recent rotation (top 10 skaters by recent points and shots). Injury tags come from the official injury report when one is available.
