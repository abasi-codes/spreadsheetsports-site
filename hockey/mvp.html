Method

How the model works

V1 · Last year's ballot

Copies last season's Hart voting.

V2 · The best player

Projects every player from his last three seasons (weighted 3-2-1, with an age curve) and scores him on what Hart voters reward.

V3 · + Team success

Projects every team. MVPs almost always come from playoff teams.

V4 · + Chaos

Simulates the season 10,000 times with career years, injuries, team swings and voter noise.

V5 · The lab

Keeps only the ideas that survived the backtest: stars stay healthier, age 33+, last year's Cup finalists and Presidents' Trophy team, position, and the vote split between two Hart-level teammates.
