Method

How the model works

V1 · Points

Points per game, compared within position (forwards vs forwards, defencemen vs defencemen).

V2 · Chances

Adds the chances a player creates for himself: individual expected goals and game score.

V3 · On the ice

Adds 5-on-5 expected-goal differential with the player on the ice vs on the bench.

V4 · Defence

Adds chances against with him on vs off the ice. 2025-26 counts double; 35+ games last season to qualify.

V5 · Deployment

Adjusts for who a player plays against and where his shifts start, from the shift charts of 2,624 NHL games.

The rule

Who can be overrated

A player has to be in the experts' top 20 in the whole league, using the average of ESPN, The Hockey News, TSN and NHL Network's preseason lists.

Gap = data rank minus expert rank. A big gap means the experts like him much more than the data does.

Players marked “young” are 21 or younger or had under 40 games in 2024-25, so their expert rank partly projects growth. Leaving them out doesn't change the top 3. Goalies are not included.
