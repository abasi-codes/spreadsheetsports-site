Method

How the model works

V1 · Points

Points per game, compared within position (forwards vs forwards, defencemen vs defencemen).

V2 · Chances

Adds individual expected goals and game score.

V3 · On the ice

Adds 5-on-5 expected-goal differential with the player on the ice vs on the bench, plus the "McDavid test": players whose numbers collapse away from a star linemate are removed.

V4 · Defence

Adds chances against with him on vs off the ice. 2025-26 counts double; 35+ games last season to qualify. Goalies are not included.
