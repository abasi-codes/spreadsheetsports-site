Method

How a season is simulated

Rate every team on chances

Expected goals from the last two seasons (score- and venue-adjusted), blended with goal differential. The standings alone carry too much luck.

Add playoff experience

Playoff series won over the last three years. It was the biggest single improvement in the backtest.

Play the real schedule

All 1,344 games of the 84-game season, with home ice, overtime and shootouts, plus random October uncertainty in every simulated season.

Playoffs, 10,000 times

Real playoff format, best-of-seven series. Preseason only: not one 2026-27 game is in the model.

Tested & rejected

Ideas that failed

  • Summer roster moves (v3)
  • Goaltending, goals saved above expected (v4)
  • The Presidents' Trophy curse
  • Cup Final hangover
  • Special teams
  • "Defence wins championships"
  • The hot goalie from last spring
  • Blending in the betting markets
  • Shot attempts (Corsi): cost one champion
  • The first few weeks of the season

Each made the backtest worse or picked no extra champions, so none of them made the final model.
